# 🔒 Acceptable Use Policy (Sample)

> This document is a sample Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for demonstration and learning purposes. It outlines guidelines for the appropriate use of organizational IT resources.

---

## 📄 Purpose
To establish clear expectations for the responsible and secure use of the organization's systems, networks, and data by all employees, contractors, and third-party users.

---

## 🧰 Scope
This policy applies to all users of the organization's IT resources, including but not limited to:
- Computers and mobile devices
- Network services
- Email and collaboration platforms (e.g., Microsoft 365)
- Cloud services and internet access

---

## ✅ Acceptable Use
Users must:
- Protect confidential and sensitive data in accordance with company policies and legal requirements
- Use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication (MFA) where applicable
- Report any suspected security incidents, phishing emails, or lost/stolen devices immediately
- Access only systems and data they are authorized to use
- Use company-provided software and tools for work purposes only

---

## ❌ Unacceptable Use
Users must NOT:
- Share login credentials or bypass security controls
- Download or install unauthorized software or cloud services
- Use company resources to engage in illegal or unethical activities
- Access or distribute explicit, discriminatory, or offensive content
- Connect unauthorized devices to the corporate network

---

## 🔐 Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including revocation of access privileges, termination, or legal action. All users are required to review and acknowledge this policy annually.
