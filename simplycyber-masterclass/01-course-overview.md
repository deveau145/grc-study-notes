## 🎯 Objective

To build foundational skills for a career in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) by exploring frameworks, risk analysis, audits, and awareness programs.

By the end of the course, students will be able to:
- Demonstrate key GRC Analyst responsibilities
- Understand how GRC supports an InfoSec program
- Perform risk assessments and audits
- Build effective security awareness content
- Contribute to GRC documentation (policies, registers, templates)
- Leverage these skills to pursue job opportunities in GRC

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

- **GRC = Enablement + Accountability.** GRC isn't just about red tape—it's about supporting the business while managing risk.
- **Frameworks = Structure.** You don't start from scratch; you align with NIST, ISO, or SOC 2 to build trust.
- **Risk Drives Everything.** Without clear risks, there’s no purpose behind controls or policies.
- **Documentation Is Power.** If it's not written, it didn’t happen—policy, risk register, awareness, audit logs all matter.
- **Awareness Programs = Measurable Impact.** Real GRC analysts deliver awareness that changes behavior, not just clicks.
- **Soft Skills Count.** Communication, documentation, and cross-department engagement are essential.
